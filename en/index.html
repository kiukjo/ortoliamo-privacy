3. Garden photos

  • Free plan: photos stay only on your phone. We do not receive them. If you change or lose your phone, or uninstall the app, they cannot be recovered.
  • Premium: a copy of the photos is saved in our archive on Cloudflare R2 (up to 1,000 photos), so you get them back on a new phone.
  • Before saving them, the app shrinks the photos and removes hidden data, including the GPS position of the shot.
  • When you delete a photo, we delete it from the server within 30 days.

4. Photo analysis with Google Gemini ("What's wrong with this plant?")

  • It works only if you ask for it, one photo at a time, and after you accept the notice shown the first time.
  • The photo is sent to our server and from there to the Google Gemini API (Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC), which analyses it and returns the result. With the photo we send only the list of crops in the catalogue and the language: we do not send your name, your email or the position of your garden.
  • We use Google's paid service: under its terms Google does not use the photos to train its models [TO CHECK: Gemini API terms for paid services at the time of publication]. Google may keep the request for a limited time for abuse checks [TO CHECK: period stated in the terms].
  • We do not keep the photo sent for analysis: we save only the text result (crop and possible problems), linked to your account, to count the month's analyses and show it to you again.
  • The result is a help and not a certain diagnosis.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time from Account › Photo analysis with Gemini › Withdraw consent: from then on no photo is sent until you give it again. Results already saved remain.

  • Deleted Premium photos: erased from the server within 30 days.

6-bis. Shared garden

If you invite someone into your garden, whoever joins sees that garden, its calendar and its journal, with the name (community nickname or the part of the email before the @) of whoever wrote each entry. Your other gardens, the photos on the server and the account data stay private. The garden data stays yours: whoever leaves or is removed no longer sees it.

7. Your rights

You can ask us at any time for:

  • access to your data;
  • correction and erasure;
  • restriction of processing;
  • portability;
  • objection;
  • withdrawal of consent, without affecting processing already carried out.

You can download all your data in a file (Account › My data) and delete the account from the app (Account › Delete account) or from our website, without the app. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it).
